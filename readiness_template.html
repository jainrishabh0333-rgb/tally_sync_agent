Basis of preparation

  1. Pending quantity per order line is the ordered quantity less the quantity already invoiced against that order number.
  2. Stock per item and size is the most recent BlncQty figure Tally has exported for that combination.
  3. Allocation. Orders are walked oldest first and each takes stock before later orders see it, so no quantity is promised twice. A given order's coverage therefore depends on the orders ahead of it.
  4. Quantities are not converted. Each figure is in that item's own billing unit — Doz, Box, Pcs, 10pcs — so any total mixes units and is a measure of scale rather than an exact count. Percentages are computed within each order and are unaffected.
  5. Exclusions. Order lines whose item and size have no recent BlncQty sighting are reported separately in section 5 and are excluded from every percentage above.
  6. Circulation. This report names parties, order numbers and shortfalls. It is for internal use and must not be sent outside the business.
